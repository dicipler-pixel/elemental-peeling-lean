# Do the gap digits drive the fission ratio? Three forms of the claim, tested

The gap digit is the last digit of a handoff's air: $4,0,6,2,8$ — five values, one per class. Your claim
can mean three different things, and they do not have the same answer. `scripts/gap_digits_fission.py`,
[`tableV11_gap_digits.csv`](tableV11_gap_digits.csv).

## A. On the prime side they do, exactly — this is the true form

$$\text{gap digit}\;\longrightarrow\;\text{class}\;\longrightarrow\;\text{the segment's own ratio}
=\frac{a}{a+4}.$$

| gap digit | class | the ratio it forces | share of handoffs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 4 | $4/8=0.500$ | 11.9% |
| 0 | 10 | $10/14=0.714$ | 23.6% |
| 6 | 16 | $16/20=0.800$ | 22.1% |
| 2 | 22 | $22/26=0.846$ | 11.1% |
| 8 | 28 | $28/32=0.875$ | 31.2% |

Checked over every one of 3,424,505 handoffs: **0 exceptions** (`air_class_of_digit` in Lean). So on the
prime side the statement is not merely true — it is forced, and the five ratios are the five digits.

**And the ladder it generates sits where the fission ratios sit.** Weighting the five rungs by their
measured shares gives

$$\sum_d \text{share}(d)\cdot\frac{a_d}{a_d+4}=0.7726,$$

against fission ratios $0.668$–$0.810$, median $0.736$. That is the level agreement, again — a number
generated by the gap digits landing inside the actinide band.

## B. Through the fragment separation: no

If the gap digits drove the fission ratio through the charge distance between the fragments, that distance
($108-Z$) would be a gap number, $\equiv4\pmod 6$:

| parent $Z$ | 90 | 92 | 93 | 94 | 95 | 96 | 98 | 100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $108-Z$ | 18 | **16** | 15 | 14 | 13 | 12 | **10** | 8 |
| $\equiv4\ (6)$? | ✗ | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ | ✗ |

2 of 8 hits where chance gives 1.33 (binomial $p=0.40$). Nothing.

## C. Through the local gap-digit composition: looks strong, is the height trend

For each of the 24 systems I took the window at its positional analogue ($\log p/\log 10^9=Z/118$,
$2\times10^{5}$ handoffs) and correlated the five digit shares with the measured offset $Z_H-54$. Raw:

| gap digit | raw $r$ with the offset | $r$ of the share with $Z$ | **partial $r$, Z removed** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | $-0.539$ ($p=0.000$) | $-0.675$ | $+0.099$ ($p=0.65$) |
| 0 | $+0.489$ ($p=0.000$) | $+0.687$ | $-0.260$ ($p=0.23$) |
| 6 | $+0.193$ ($p=0.000$) | $+0.363$ | $-0.243$ ($p=0.26$) |
| **2** | $\mathbf{-0.675}$ ($p=0.000$) | $-0.816$ | $\mathbf{+0.076}$ ($p=0.73$) |
| 8 | $+0.458$ ($p=0.000$) | $+0.272$ | $+0.452$ ($p=0.029$) |

Every raw correlation is "significant", and **every one of them is the height trend**: each digit share is
itself nearly a function of height ($|r|$ up to 0.82), and $Z$ alone explains **73%** of the offset's
variance. Remove that and the largest collapses from $r=-0.675$ to $r=+0.076$ — sign flipped, $p=0.73$.

This is the exact trap I flagged before the test: the fission offset is a function of $Z$, height is a
function of the window position, so *any* monotone-in-height statistic will "correlate" with the fission
ratio. The control that kills it is the partial correlation, and it kills all five (one survives weakly at
$p=0.029$, which does not clear a five-digit Bonferroni threshold of $0.01$).

## Verdict

| form of the claim | verdict |
|---|---|
| gap digits drive the **prime-side** ratio | **true, and forced** — digit → class → $a/(a+4)$, 0 exceptions |
| the gap-digit ladder's mean lands in the fission band | **true** — 0.7726 against 0.668–0.810 |
| gap digits drive the fission ratio **through the fragment separation** | **no** — 2 of 8, $p=0.40$ |
| gap digits drive it **through the local composition** | **no** — every raw correlation is the $Z$ trend; partial $r$ collapses |
| so the gap digits *drive* the fission ratio | **not as a mechanism.** They set the level, not the variation |

The sharpest way to say it: **the gap digits generate the ratio ladder, and the fission ratios live on that
ladder's range and near its mean — but the variation across systems is 73% parent charge and the rest
excitation, with nothing left for the digits once those are accounted for.**
