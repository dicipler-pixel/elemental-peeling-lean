ABSTRACT

Four separately published or separately written bodies of work by the same author -- a
periodic table read as chains of removal energies, a loop wall in a reflection coordinate,
a theory of predictive states and their clock, and a treatment of Ramanujan's gamma
fractions as stacks of faces -- are usually described as related.  This paper says exactly
how, and no more.  All four are written in one small coordinate: a positive ratio z, the
reflection
Gamma = (z - 1)/(z + 1) = tanh((1/2) log z), the value Gamma^2 = 1/2, and the rule that
reflections compose by adding their rapidities.  Three interfaces between them are compiled
rather than asserted.

Two consequences are not in the individual papers.  A window of peel steps can cross the wall
with every one of its steps inside it, because reflections add; in the shipped archive 91,638
of 91,768 crossing windows are of that kind, while no window crosses on geometric mean.  And
the archive cannot select the value 3 + 2*sqrt(2): every cutoff in an interval around it
classifies all 3,570 steps identically, and the aggregate counts that look like confirmation
are computed from the cutoff rather than measuring it.

The contribution is therefore a checked artifact and a set of limits: about 1,600 named
results in one build, three compiled interfaces, a corrigenda list of statements that turned
out false, and a computation showing what the evidence does not establish.  No physical
mechanism is derived and no predictive power over chemistry is claimed.


1.  INTRODUCTION

The papers this one draws on were written separately and each has its own scope statements.
Reading them together, one notices the same three lines of algebra in all of them: a ratio
turned into a reflection, a distinguished value of that reflection, and an addition law.  The
temptation is to call that an identification.  It is not.  What is true is narrower and can
be stated exactly:

    the developments share a coordinate, and three of the interfaces between them are
    compiled: the two predictive quotients are proved to be the same subspace, one
    development's path-descent theorem is specialised to another's ledger, and two peel steps
    are proved to compose by the velocity law.

Everything else is a reading.  The continued fraction's terms being these faces is a reading,
and the knot involution being an instance of the generic oddness lemma is a reading.  Section 3
says which is which, sentence by sentence, and Section 8 says what is consequently not
claimed.

The paper makes two further points of substance.  Section 4 shows that the data layer is
almost entirely a story about composition, and Section 4.3 shows that the same data cannot
select the constant the composition story is organized around.  The second is the more
defensible result and the less welcome one.


2.  THE COORDINATE

2.1  Ratio and reflection.

Take a ladder of positive readings I_1, I_2, ... -- removal energies of one atom, or any other
ordered positive sequence.  A step is z = I_{k+1}/I_k, and its reflection is

    Gamma = (z - 1)/(z + 1) = tanh( (1/2) log z ).                                 (2.1)

The identity is the logarithmic form of artanh, DLMF 4.37.24; the inverse z = (1+Gamma)/(1-Gamma)
is the Cayley coordinate.  Reversal sends z to 1/z and Gamma to -Gamma, so Gamma^2 is
reversal-invariant and Gamma is the only reversal-odd part of the datum.  (proved:
LoopWall/Ledger.lean, gam_eq_tanh_half_log, gam_reverse, gam_sq_reverse; a second independent
proof of (2.1) is unified/VOTKP/Smith.lean, smith_identity.)

2.2  The wall.

One value is distinguished: Gamma^2 = 1/2.  Over positive ratios it is exactly
z = 3 + 2*sqrt(2) = 5.828427... or its reciprocal 3 - 2*sqrt(2).  In ledger units, where
r = log z is the rapidity, the wall is r = +/- 2 log(1 + sqrt(2)).  (proved:
LoopWall/Wall.lean, wall_eq_iff; unified/Bridge/Reflection.lean, wall_ledger_value.)

The name is a definition.  In the lossless two-channel normalisation the reflected and
transmitted shares are Gamma^2 and 1 - Gamma^2, and at Gamma^2 = 1/2 they coincide; nothing
here fixes whether Gamma is an amplitude or a power.  What gives the value weight is that it
keeps marking where a sum of steps changes qualitative behaviour.

2.3  Composition.

Rapidities add.  A window of steps has ratio the product of its ratios, and in the reflection
coordinate this is the velocity-addition law

    Gamma(z1 z2) = Gamma(z1) (+) Gamma(z2),   u (+) v = (u+v)/(1+uv),               (2.2)

which is DLMF 4.35.3 in the tangent-half-angle variable.  Hence:

    (a) a window exceeds the upper wall 3 + 2*sqrt(2) exactly when the sum of its steps'
        rapidities exceeds log(3 + 2*sqrt(2)); no single step need cross;
    (b) the geometric mean of steps that are each inside the wall is inside the wall, for
        any positive ladder, because a mean is bounded by its largest term.

    (proved: unified/Bridge/Composition.lean -- composite_reflection_eq, composite_two_eq_oplus,
    crosses_iff_ledger_sum, two_halves_cross, geomean_lt_wall,
    composition_crosses_mean_does_not.)

Statement (a) is the application to a ladder of the elementary fact that a product exceeds a
threshold exactly when the sum of logarithms does (DLMF 4.8.2 with monotonicity); statement
(b) is the equally elementary bound on a mean.  Neither is a new criterion, and Section 7
does not claim one.  What is new is that they are the right way to read the data.


3.  WHERE IT APPLIES

3.1  Elemental peeling.

Read an element as the ladder of its successive ionisation energies.  The shipped archive has
118 elements and 5,714 steps, of which the hydrogen-to-astatine baseline is 85 elements and
3,570 steps.  Eight of the baseline steps lie outside the wall, and every one is the first
opening of a noble-gas core: Li at k=1, Be at 2, B at 3, C at 4 (helium); Na at 1 (neon); K at
1 (argon); Rb at 1 (krypton); Cs at 1 (xenon).  Sixty-five further "anion rungs" -- the ratio
from the electron affinity to the first ionisation energy -- give 53 more values outside.
(measured: scripts/atlas_census.py.)

That is exact arithmetic.  As an explanation it is a restatement: a core opening costs much
more than the removal that follows, which is what "core opening" means.

3.2  Dirac time.

The predictive quotient -- the state space modulo everything no future reading can see -- is
the finite-dimensional observability quotient of linear systems theory: the unobservable
subspace is the intersection of the kernels of R L^n, the quotient is by it, and its dimension
is the rank of the observability map.  Two developments prove this independently, and the two
versions are the same subspace, Cayley-Hamilton being the bridge.  (proved:
unified/Bridge/Quotient.lean, unseen_eq_unobservable.)

The same work gives the gap law mu_j - mu_k = tau_dot (E_j - E_k) for a generator with
eigen-data (proved: unified/DiracTime/CommonClock.lean, common_gap_scaling).  Read as a peel
rate this says the frequency of the peel flow is the clock rate times the energy gap.  For a
real gap delta and the zero-offset flow Gamma(tau) = tanh(delta tau / 2), the squared wall has
exactly one forward solution precisely when delta is nonzero, namely

    |delta| * tau_wall = 2 artanh(1/sqrt(2)) = log(3 + 2*sqrt(2)),                  (3.1)

and a zero gap has none.  The hypotheses matter: with a nonzero offset, Gamma(tau) = tanh(1 +
tau/2) starts above 1/sqrt(2) and never returns.  (proved:
unified/Found/CommonClock.lean, forward_wall_time_mul_abs_gap, existsUnique_forward_wall_iff.)

The identification of the gap with the peel rate is a model identification, not a derivation of
either from a mechanism, and it is marked as such in the ledger.

3.3  VOTKP.

The same coordinate with the composition law at the centre: the Smith coordinate, the velocity
law (2.2), the film echo, the exact circle deciding whether a thin film raises or lowers the
returned power.  Of the four, this is the development whose own theorem is (2.2) stated for the
peel.

3.4  Ramanujan's gamma fractions.

The gamma quotient is

    4 Gamma((x+3)/4)^2 / Gamma((x+1)/4)^2 = x + 1^2/(2x + 3^2/(2x + 5^2/(2x + ...))).

Its n-th term, as a transfer matrix, is [[0, (2n-1)^2], [1, 2x]] with characteristic roots
x +/- s, s = sqrt(x^2 + (2n-1)^2); that is the standard continued-fraction matrix (DLMF
1.12(ii); Paydon-Wall 1942; Kisil, arXiv:1412.1457, section 2), and the assignment of terms to
matrices is a derivation in the session record, not formalised here.  What is proved here is
the algebra of the face that derivation names.  Put

    z_n = (s + x)/(s - x),   Gamma_n = x / sqrt(x^2 + (2n-1)^2);

then Gamma_n = (z_n - 1)/(z_n + 1) exactly, and Gamma_n^2 = 1/2 exactly when 2n - 1 = x.  Since
2n - 1 runs over the positive odd integers, an odd x has a face exactly on the wall and an even
x does not; Brouncker's fraction (x = 1) has it at the first face, with z_1 = 3 + 2*sqrt(2).
(proved*: unified/Bridge/Faces.lean -- the asterisk means proved about the definitions above,
not about the continued fraction.  That the fraction's terms are these faces is a reading.)

The session record reports that the truncation error falls like N^(-x), that |error| N^x tends
to 4/pi^2 at x = 1 and to 1/4 at x = 3, and that error times the product of the face ratios
stays nearly fixed.  This repository reproduces the finite evidence only: at N = 6400 the
script gives 0.4053049 and 0.24999999 against 4/pi^2 = 0.4052847 and 1/4, agreement to five
figures and not a proof of a limit; and the attenuation product varies by 0.3 per cent at x = 1
and x = 3 while the error falls four orders of magnitude, and by 5 per cent at x = 1/2.
(measured, finite: scripts/ramanujan_faces.py.  The two limits are open.)

4.  WHAT THE DATA SAY

4.1  The census.

Every published count in the periodic table and the scale atlas reproduces from the shipped
JSON by one script: the 3,570 steps and their eight outliers; the 65 rungs and 53; the 83 of 83
elements whose largest step opens a core (78 helium, 2 neon, 1 each argon, krypton, xenon);
5,714 steps in the full archive; 183,770 contiguous windows of at least two steps, of which
91,768 exceed the wall and 91,638 do so with every constituent step inside; zero above the
wall on geometric mean; 183,081 windows with both endpoint widths usable and 689 without; the
largest two- and three-step means 4.76566745 (Li) and 3.15005083 (B).  (measured:
scripts/atlas_census.py.)

The rule that makes the window count exact is stated in the atlas's prose and easy to miss:
every intervening charge must be present, so a window is a run of consecutive charge labels
and not a slice of the step list.  Dropping only that requirement, with ratios from the
endpoint energies, gives 191,817 / 98,572 / 98,442; dropping it and switching to products of
the rounded stored ratios gives 191,817 / 95,569 / 95,439.  The main triple is the same under
both ratio conventions, so it is the consecutive-charge rule that does the work.

4.2  Composition, not fine-tuning.

Of the 91,768 crossing windows, 91,638 have every constituent step inside the wall: 99.86 per
cent.  The composition theorem explains how each of those is possible; it does not imply the
number, which depends on this archive's window lengths and ratio distribution.  Addition makes
accumulated crossings possible, and this archive is full of them.  The remaining 130 windows
contain an outside step and cross trivially.  A summary that keeps only each step's
inside/outside label cannot recover the other 91,638; the products of the tabulated ratios can.

4.3  What the table cannot select.

Order the 3,570 baseline step ratios.  The largest inside the wall is 5.639665 (nitrogen, k=5)
and the smallest outside is 5.947101 (caesium, k=1).  Every cutoff in that interval classifies
all 3,570 steps identically, and 3 + 2*sqrt(2) = 5.828427 lies inside it.  Adding the 65 anion
rungs narrows the interval to [5.807902, 5.866539) -- silver's rung the largest inside,
silicon's the smallest outside -- which still contains the wall.  (measured:
scripts/atlas_census.py.)

Nor do the aggregate counts rescue it.  The 91,768 and 91,638 are deterministic functions of
the cutoff: at c = 5.85, inside the band, the per-step classification is unchanged, still
eight outside and all core openings, but the crossing count falls to 91,616 and the
accumulated-only count to 91,486.  The point is not that a computed quantity is never
evidence; it is that these counts have no independently measured target in the archive, so
reproducing their published values cannot select the cutoff.  The raw endpoint ratios are
cutoff-independent; which compositions count as accumulated-only is not.

4.4  A shape comparison.

The table also reports that reading each element's first eight steps on other shapes --
triangle, square, diamond, hexagon -- groups elements by column far better than the raw
energies do.  On the archive's 77-element comparison the scores are circle 53, diamond 52,
cube 48, simplex 60 and raw energies 4, with simplex numerically highest, a simplex-minus-circle
gap of 7 of 77.  The scoring target and the evaluation population are fixed by that comparison
and nothing beyond it is inferred.  (measured: scripts/shapes_recall.py.)  The table's further
claim that no shape beats the circle by more than its bootstrap spread is not reproducible:
no resampling unit, replicate count, seed or spread is shipped.


5.  REVERSAL, SIGN, AND WHAT THE WALL CANNOT SEE

Reversal sends z to 1/z and Gamma to -Gamma, so any readout factoring through Gamma^2 cannot
distinguish a ratio from its reciprocal or the two branches of the wall.  Two statements must
be kept apart here:

    a single ratio fixed by reversal has z = 1 and Gamma = 0, and is at neither branch;
    a ladder fixed by reversal as a sequence -- palindromic readings -- need not have
    Gamma = 0 anywhere.  The readings 1, W, 1 with W = 3 + 2*sqrt(2) are unchanged by
    reversal and both of their steps are exactly at the wall.

The first is proved (unified/Bridge/Chirality.lean, mirror_fixed_iff_gam_eq_zero).  The second
is the reason the distinction has to be made in words; an earlier version of this material
conflated them.

What the wall hides is the sign.  Each branch has nonzero Gamma, and a Gamma^2 readout reports
the same number at +Gamma and -Gamma.  That is blindness to the sign of one coordinate and it
is all that is proved; Gamma itself and log z are computed throughout the papers and are
reversal-sensitive.  No further notion of chirality is formalised here.

The same elementary argument -- an invariant odd under an involution vanishes on the fixed
locus -- occurs in the other developments: a chiral pairing S T S^-1 = -T pairs the spectrum
and kills odd moments, and theta(mirror K) = -theta(K) makes theta vanish on amphichiral
knots.  It is an analogy among proofs, not a compiled three-way interface.  What is proved is
the generic lemma, an abstract theta schema whose K, mirror and theta are arbitrary, and the
LoopWall reversal identity; the Dirac spectral theorem is a separate argument and no knot
invariant is formalised.  (proved: odd_involution_vanishes, theta_vanishes_on_amphichiral;
not compiled as instances: Dirac, knots.)

The lemma gives one direction only.  Over all 12,965 prime knots through 13 crossings, KnotInfo
release 2026.11.2 has 78 amphicheiral (47 negative, 30 fully, 1 positive) and the name set is
identical to the 78 with theta = 0; there are none at 11 or at 13 crossings.  That is a complete
finite verification and still not a proof of the converse, which is a statement about all
knots.  A single odd function vanishing away from its fixed locus -- x^3 - x at x = 1 -- shows
oddness alone can never yield the converse.  (measured: scripts/knot_census.py over two shipped
lists.  proved: odd_does_not_imply_converse.  open: the converse.)


6.  WHAT IS NOT ESTABLISHED

The negative results are part of the content, and they delimit the positive ones.

    6.1  Composition does not select the ledger readout.  Evenness, vanishing at zero balance
         and append-additivity of a channel sum leave both the log ledger and the Gamma^2
         readout admissible; selection needs additivity of a determinant readout plus
         regularity.  (proved: append_additivity_alone_does_not_select_ledger;
         CompositionBridge.eq_const_mul_log_of_append_additive.)
    6.2  Twelve statements transcribed from earlier prose are false as written and are kept as
         refutation theorems beside their corrected forms.  (proved: FalseControls/;
         LIMITATIONS.md.)
    6.3  The predictive quotient of a peel ladder is one-dimensional in the scalar present-
         reading model, and model-dependent rather than intrinsic: other linearisations of the
         same ladder separate the wall, and one changes dimension at z = 1 instead.
         (proved: Bridge/Quotient.lean; Found/PredictivePeel.lean.)
    6.4  The block determinant identity is local: 1 - Gamma^2 = 4 det p holds for one
         two-channel block; the composition bridge's blockDet is a product over blocks and
         equals (1 - Gamma^2)^n.  Two wall blocks give 1/4, not 1/2.
         (proved: Bridge/Reflection.lean; Found/WallReadout.lean.)
    6.5  Perfect reflection is unattained: det p = 0 corresponds to |Gamma| = 1, and for a
         positive ratio |Gamma| < 1 strictly.  (proved: Found/WallReadout.lean.)

These are not evidence for the positive statements.  Assurance for each positive statement
comes separately, from its cited proof or its reproducible measurement; the corrigenda neither
add to that nor substitute for it.


7.  PRIOR ART

Riccati equations for nonuniform transmission lines were standard long before this work.
Pierce gave the impedance Riccati equation and its uniform-line tanh solution in 1943 (Bell
System Technical Journal 22, 263-265); Walker and Wax gave the voltage-reflection form in 1946
(Journal of Applied Physics 17, 1043-1045).  The particular solution
Gamma = tanh(omega t / 2) follows from DLMF 4.34.3 with 4.35.12 and needs no transmission-line
attribution.  The identity (2.1) is DLMF 4.37.24.  The addition law is DLMF 4.35.3, which is
also the relativistic velocity law in the tangent-half-angle variable.  The continued-fraction
step matrix and its recurrence are standard (DLMF 1.12(ii); Paydon and Wall, Duke Math. J. 9,
1942; Kisil, arXiv:1412.1457).  The predictive quotient is the observability quotient of
linear systems theory.  None of this is claimed as new.

What this paper offers is not an algebra and not a criterion:

    (i)  a proof-level synthesis of four named works by this author, with the duplicate
         repository proofs kept and their interfaces stated as theorems -- an integration of
         my own repositories, not a new algebraic object.  The four, with versions: the
         loop-wall development in this repository; "The Dirac Time of the Gigantefermion"
         (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22978630); "VOTKP: The Vector of Traits Kept under Peeling"
         (v2.9, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23340757); and the Ramanujan-faces session record of
         13-15 November 2026.  The periodic table v2.2 supplies the data layer.
    (ii) the application to removal-step ladders of the elementary product/log and mean
         bounds, with the archive counts and their Lean formalisation;
    (iii) the restatement in peel notation of the eigenvalues of the standard continued-
         fraction matrix: with r = x/(2n-1), z_n = exp(2 asinh r) and
         Gamma_n = tanh(asinh r) = x/sqrt(x^2 + (2n-1)^2), whence Gamma_n^2 = 1/2 exactly
         when 2n - 1 = x.  I have not located this displayed peel formulation elsewhere and
         claim only the formulation and the odd-x observation;
    (iv) the itemised corrigenda, and the computation that this archive leaves an interval of
         cutoffs with identical per-step classifications while yielding different aggregate
         counts inside it.  Both are repository- and archive-specific.

The last is the most defensible contribution and the least exciting: a checked statement of
what the evidence does not show.


8.  SCOPE

No physical mechanism is derived.  No Hamiltonian, force, optical constant or ionisation energy
is produced, and the one rate identification in Section 3.2 is marked as an identification.
No predictive power over chemistry is claimed: the body sorts ratios that were already
measured and has not predicted one that was not.  The wall value is not selected from data
(Section 4.3).  The knot converse is open.  Theta is not formalised, and neither is the continued
fraction.  The developments were not one
programme; the unification is retrospective.

A reader who wants the shortest true description: four developments share a coordinate, three
interfaces between them are compiled, one table is consistent with the coordinate's constant
but cannot select it, and the negative results are the part most likely to be confirmed by
someone else's checking.
